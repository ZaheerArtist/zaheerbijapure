Reel One

The Apprentice

2014 — 2017

Fade in.

Int. Vertex Volt Studio — Mumbai — Day — 2014

A young artist sits at a workstation with Maya open. He rigs, models, textures and animates whatever lands on his desk, from Indian TV epics like Devon Ke Dev Mahadev and Hatim to TV commercials.

Zaheer (V.O.)

Animation taught me timing. Every frame has to earn its place.

Int. Tata Elxsi — Day — 2016

He animates and lays out shots for the French series Maya the Bee, a Tata Motors ad and eLearning films. Evenings go to a post-graduate diploma in Advertising and Media. It will matter later.

3D Animator & 3D ArtistVertex Volt · 2014 – 2015
Animator & CG Layout ArtistTata Elxsi · 2016 – 2017

Cut to:

Reel Two

The Big Screen

2017 — 2022

Int. Red Chillies Entertainment — Night — 2017

His first feature, Zero. He learns body tracking, locking a digital body onto a real actor frame by frame until no one can see the join.

Int. DNEG — Mumbai — 2018

The studio behind Hollywood blockbusters. He tracks bodies and cameras, manages layout on Devotion, pre-delivers complex shots for The Matrix Resurrections, and solves the flying and water sequences nobody else wants.

Zaheer (V.O.)

(quietly)

If the audience notices my work, I've failed. If they believe the shot, I've done my job.

Soon he is teaching it too, as the studio's upskill trainer.

Animator & Body Track ArtistRed Chillies · 2017 – 2018
Layout · Senior Body Track · Matchmove · Upskill TrainerDNEG · 2018 – 2022

Reel Three

Behind the Camera

2022 — 2025

Int. MPC — Layout Department — 2022

Senior now. He builds the cameras, previz and techviz for films like The Flash, Kraven the Hunter and Back in Action. He dresses sets, builds proxies and makes sure every department downstream gets what it needs.

The studio notices. Twice.

Senior VFX Layout · Previz · Techviz · MatchmoveMPC · 2022 – 2025
Winner · Dec 2023The Spotlight AwardMPC · Technicolor Creative Studios
Winner · Feb 2023Star Performer AwardMPC · Technicolor Creative Studios

Reel Four

The Plot Twist

2025 — Now

Int. Studio Blo — Mumbai — 2025

The tools change overnight. Images and video can be generated from a sentence. Most artists panic. He sees cameras, lenses and continuity, the same craft he has practised for a decade. He becomes AI VFX Lead.

Ext. Dubai skyline — Sunset — Sep 2025

A new city. He joins Saatchi & Saatchi ME, part of Publicis Groupe, as Senior Designer, AI Creative. Remember that advertising diploma? This is where the story pays it off.

Zaheer (V.O.)

I write a prompt the way I'd brief a shot. The tool is new. The storytelling isn't.

AI VFX LeadStudio Blo · Apr – Jul 2025
Senior Designer, AI CreativeSaatchi & Saatchi ME · UAE · Sep 2025 – Present
