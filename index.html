The person behind the frames

I treat AI like a camera department: every frame is directed, then finished with the care of a VFX shot.

I started in 2014 as a 3D animator on Indian TV, then moved into feature-film VFX: body tracking on Zero at Red Chillies, layout and matchmove at DNEG on The Matrix Resurrections and Devotion, and senior layout and previz at MPC on The Flash and Kraven the Hunter, where I won two awards. Today I bring that craft to AI at Saatchi & Saatchi MEA in Dubai, working with creative and strategy teams to turn ideas into cinematic work for brands.
