A storyteller working across AI, VFX and content. I craft cinematic campaigns from concept to screen, built on more than a decade of making shots for Hollywood and Indian films.

The story

I started in 2014 as a 3D animator on Indian TV and ads, then moved into body tracking on Zero at Red Chillies. At DNEG and MPC I worked in layout, matchmove, previz and techviz on films like The Matrix Resurrections, Devotion, The Flash and Kraven the Hunter, and trained teams as an upskill trainer along the way.

In 2025 I took that craft into AI: first as AI VFX Lead at Studio Blo, and now as AI Senior Designer at Saatchi & Saatchi ME, where I turn ideas into AI-driven images and films for brands, working closely with creative and strategy teams.
